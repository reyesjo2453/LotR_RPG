# Gemini prompt — 5 level-composition variations

Attach: boss.png, crypt.png, ruin.png (real in-game renders) + the asset sheets.

Create 5 DIFFERENT concept paintings of one dungeon room for a dark-fantasy action game, in the exact
camera and style of the attached screenshots: orthographic isometric view, 30 degree pitch, 45 degree yaw,
stylised hand-painted cel-shaded look with thick warm-gold rim light on stone edges, cold teal rune glow,
deep charcoal stone. Keep the floor tile pattern, the fluted rune pillars and the prop designs from the
attachments (broken pillar, rubble pile, fallen column, sarcophagus, rune obelisk, kneeling knight statue,
stone throne, iron cage, offering bowl, barrel stack, blue brazier).

Show the FULL room, not just floor: all four walls visible with painted stone-brick facades, cornice,
rune columns and occasional arched alcoves, a floor edge/lip, and a corner where two walls meet.
Light only from braziers (warm amber pools) and rune glow (teal); wide dark vignette; light dust in the air.
No UI, no text, no characters except one small hero at room centre for scale.

Variations (one image each):
1. BOSS HALL - throne on a raised dais against the far wall, two knight statues, obelisks flanking, large open centre.
2. CRYPT - rows of sarcophagi along both side walls, one open coffin, offering bowl, cold teal dominant light.
3. RUINED GALLERY - collapsed roof section, broken pillars, rubble, fallen column, a shaft of pale light, amber braziers.
4. ARMORY / STOREROOM - barrel stacks, iron cages, crates, torch-lit warm palette, narrow aisles.
5. UNSEEN REALM - the same ruined room in the spectral realm: translucent blue arch, ghost brazier, desaturated stone, floating motes.
